## Data Display Preference (For Any Data-Heavy Product)

If this product displays data (records, items, users, orders, metrics, etc.), choose a display pattern. Tables are NOT an option.

**Display Pattern:** [Choose one or describe your own]
- **Card Palette** — Grid of visual cards, each showing primary data + status/indicator. Click to inspect. Best for: browsable collections, items with status.
- **Master-Detail Slide-Over** — Card palette on left, slide-over panel on right with full details + CRUD actions. Best for: management UIs, CRUD apps.
- **Pipeline / Kanban** — Columns by status stage, cards move between them. Best for: workflows, status-driven data.
- **Rich List** — Horizontal rows with avatar/icon, primary text, secondary text, status pill, inline actions. NOT a table. Best for: dense data, feeds.
- **Metric Dashboard** — Large cards with sparklines, trend indicators, comparison periods. Best for: numeric/analytics data.

## Anti-Slop Checklist

Before proceeding, confirm ALL of these. A "No" on any = redesign before coding.

### Identity
- [ ] Is this a brand-first or product-first interface?
- [ ] Would a designer identify this product's industry/audience from design alone?
- [ ] Does at least one design choice surprise in a good way?

### Visual Blacklists
- [ ] Is the typeface something other than Inter, Roboto, Arial, or Space Grotesk?
- [ ] Is the color scheme something other than purple/blue gradient on white?
- [ ] Is the hero section something other than centered heading + paragraph + 2 buttons?
- [ ] Are features presented asymmetrically, not as a 3-column card grid?

### Imagery
- [ ] Does every section have real imagery (not just colored boxes)?
- [ ] Is the hero section visually rich (photo, illustration, or product UI)?
- [ ] Are placeholder images from unsplash/pexels (not empty rectangles)?

### Data Display
- [ ] Are data displayed as cards, kanban, rich list, or metrics — NOT tables?
- [ ] Is there a slide-over panel or detail view for inspecting individual records?
- [ ] If dashboard: are there charts/graphs and sparklines on metric cards?

### Layout
- [ ] Is there a top navbar with navigation and user avatar (for app/dashboard UIs)?
- [ ] Is there NO search input in the navbar?
- [ ] Does the layout restructure on mobile, not just shrink?

### Animation (Mandatory)
- [ ] Do all cards/items animate on hover (lift + shadow)?
- [ ] Do all buttons animate on hover and press?
- [ ] Do lists/grids stagger in on page load?
- [ ] Do modals/drawers/toasts animate open and closed?
- [ ] Do state changes (loading, toggle, tab) animate?
- [ ] Is prefers-reduced-motion handled?
- [ ] If landing page: scroll reveals, text masks, image reveals present?

### Content
- [ ] Is demo content realistic (no Lorem ipsum, no "Feature 1")?
- [ ] Are there no fake testimonials, metrics, or logos?
